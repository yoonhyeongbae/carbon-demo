# EV Carbon Optimizer v0.5.8

This update is designed for the EVCO v0.5.6 DEV_READY package and includes:

1. Shared result-map filters for Stage 1 / Stage 1→2 / Stage 2 / Stage 2→3.
2. Shared material filters for every active material in the current product structure.
3. Scope 1+2 / Scope 3 visualization only in the Results tab (duplicate removed from Analysis).
4. Removal of CARTO / OpenStreetMap hosted basemap tiles.
5. Offline Natural Earth 1:110m Admin-0 country polygons as the basemap.
6. A lightweight map renderer: no map-level mouse-move callback, fewer route points, one direction arrow per route, and keep_alive enabled.
7. Legal/release documentation templates for software-business status management, computer-program copyright registration, distribution, and commercial sale.

## Map data

Natural Earth Admin 0 – Countries, 1:110m, version 5.1.1. Natural Earth states that its raster and vector map data are public domain and may be modified, electronically disseminated, and used commercially without permission. ## Important legal distinction

There is no universal Korean government filing called a mandatory "software product registration" for an ordinary privately distributed desktop application. The software-business item in the legal packet is the optional `소프트웨어사업자 일반 현황 관리` process under Software Promotion Act Article 58 and its Enforcement Rule Article 17. Copyright registration is a separate process handled under the Copyright Act and Korea Copyright Commission procedures.
